Measurement details

“Before the shift” is the median pressure during the preceding one second of Forward. “Lowest in Neutral” is the minimum before the next recorded gear change. The duration describes the longest continuous spell at that minimum reading, estimated by holding each received value until the next sample.

Readings are approximately 0.1 seconds apart. The reported duration describes the received data and its resolution. How this comparison was made

461 indexed log files were scanned, with transmission data on 26 dates. The cruise comparison uses Forward gear, at least 1,800 RPM and fresh speed-over-ground readings of at least 15 knots, sustained for 30 seconds. The ten-second pressure-variation comparison uses windows with RPM varying by no more than 100.

The Forward-to-Neutral comparison uses the median pressure in the preceding one second of Forward and the minimum during the initial uninterrupted Neutral interval. It stops at the next gear change, a message gap longer than one second, or ten seconds. Pressure was lower in 2,381 of 3,394 Port intervals and 2,405 of 3,349 Starboard intervals.

These operating selections are analysis rules. Manufacturer-defined pressure limits and alarm timing remain to be confirmed. No independent alarm timestamp has been matched to an individual recorded transition.
